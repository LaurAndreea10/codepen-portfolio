Concepts and initial drafts were generated with AI assistance in Canva, followed by text, formula and link corrections. The collection presents materials and design directions; it is not a Canva certification or evidence that every element was built manually.

The case study includes fields for documenting personal refinements. Links open Canva in a new tab; viewing or editing may require sign-in.
